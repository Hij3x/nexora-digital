01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data that a website can store through your browser. They can help websites remember information about a visit and provide certain functionality.

Similar technologies, such as local storage, may also be used to store limited information on your device.

02

How Nexora May Use Cookies

Depending on the features enabled on our website, Nexora may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping website features working.
  • Supporting secure account sessions.
  • Remembering certain preferences.
  • Protecting the website against abuse.
  • Understanding website performance.
  • Improving the user experience.
03

Essential Cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be necessary for features of the website to function properly.

For example, essential technologies may be used for authentication, security, session management or remembering actions necessary to provide a service you requested.

Essential functionality

Disabling technologies that are strictly necessary for a feature may prevent that feature from working correctly.

04

Preference Technologies

Nexora may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember choices you make, such as interface preferences or settings.

These technologies can make future visits more convenient by reducing the need to select the same preferences repeatedly.

05

Analytics

Nexora may use analytics technologies in the future to better understand how visitors interact with the website.

Analytics information may help us understand things such as page usage, website performance and general interaction patterns.

Current website development

The technologies used by Nexora may change as the website develops. Where required, appropriate controls or consent options can be provided before optional analytics or advertising technologies are enabled.

06

Third-Party Services

Certain Nexora features may rely on third-party providers for services such as hosting, authentication, payments, communications or analytics.

Those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their respective policies and the configuration of the services used by Nexora.

07

Your Cookie Choices

Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through browser settings.

If Nexora introduces optional technologies that require a website-level choice, we may also provide a cookie preference tool that allows you to manage those choices.

Blocking certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

08

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, technology providers or use of cookies changes.

When changes are made, the updated version may be posted on this page with a revised update date.

09

Questions About Cookies

If you have questions about how Nexora uses cookies or similar technologies, contact us.
