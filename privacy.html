01

Overview

Nexora provides website design, development, hosting, maintenance and related digital services. This Privacy Policy describes the types of personal information that may be collected when you interact with Nexora and how that information may be used.

By using our website or voluntarily providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Nexora, we may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Account and client portal information.
  • Information about a website or project you ask us to create.
  • Messages, support requests and other communications you send to us.
  • Billing and transaction information.

Our website may also receive certain technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address and website activity when those technologies are enabled.

03

How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Nexora to:

  • Provide and manage requested services.
  • Create and administer client accounts.
  • Communicate about projects and support.
  • Prepare quotes, invoices and billing information.
  • Operate, maintain and improve our websites and services.
  • Protect our services against abuse, fraud or security threats.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
04

Payments

When online payment features are available, payments may be processed through a third-party payment provider.

Payment security

Nexora does not intend to directly store complete payment-card numbers or card security codes on its own website systems. Payment information may instead be handled by the payment processor used to complete the transaction.

05

When Information May Be Shared

Information may be provided to service providers when reasonably necessary to operate Nexora or provide requested services. These may include providers involved in hosting, authentication, communications, analytics, payments or other technology services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights or property of Nexora, our users or others.

We do not intend to sell personal information to advertisers.

06

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Nexora may use cookies or similar browser technologies where needed for functionality, security, preferences or analytics.

More information about these technologies will be available in our Cookie Policy .

07

Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

No website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also protect their passwords and account credentials.

08

Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, administer accounts, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and satisfy applicable legal requirements.

09

Your Choices

You may contact Nexora if you have questions about personal information we maintain about you or if you would like to request an appropriate correction or other available privacy request.

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, accounting or legitimate operational purposes.

10

Children's Information

Nexora's services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children through the website without appropriate authorization where required.

If you believe information involving a child has been submitted improperly, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Nexora's services, technologies or legal obligations change. When the policy is updated, the revised version and its updated date may be posted on this page.

12

Contact Us

Questions or requests relating to this Privacy Policy can be sent to Nexora.
